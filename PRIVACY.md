# Replay Scout — Privacy policy

Replay Scout does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- It runs only on www.duelingbook.com, and only when you open its panel and press Rebuild.
- It downloads the replays you paste and card artworks from Duelingbook, the same way the Duelingbook replay page does.
- Pasted links and downloaded card artworks are kept only in your browser (extension storage), to save you work next time.
- There are no analytics, no tracking and no servers of ours: nothing leaves your browser except the requests to Duelingbook.

Replay Scout is not affiliated with Duelingbook.
